Scenario, framework and valuation inputs

Opening FY2025A balances are in the OPEN constant of the script. Under Ind AS the opening balance sheet adds a right-of-use asset and lease liability of Rs 40 Cr each (no equity effect); under AS lease rent stays in other expenses. Other-expense % includes lease rent; Ind AS removes it from opex and shows depreciation and interest instead.

Cash flow follows the indirect method of AS 3 / Ind AS 7 (interest paid and dividends in financing, interest received in investing). Deferred tax follows AS 22 / Ind AS 12 (timing difference between tax and book depreciation). Under Ind AS, receivables carry an expected credit loss provision (Ind AS 109) and leases follow Ind AS 116. Working-capital borrowings act as a revolver: they draw when cash would fall below the minimum cash balance and repay from surplus. Interest can use average balances (iterated, no circular formulas).

Key bank ratios

Format follows the CMA data (Forms II-VI) used by Indian banks under the Tandon / Chore / Nayak Committee recommendations, with line items drawn from Schedule III heads. Banks often issue their own CMA template, and Form I (existing and proposed limits) needs your sanction details, so it is not generated. Current maturities of term loans are shown in current liabilities. Deferred tax liability is shown under term liabilities. Ratio colours test against common indicative norms (current ratio 1.33, TOL/TNW 3.0, DSCR 1.25, interest cover 2.0), which differ by bank and industry. Audited FY2025A is sample data; confirm treatment with your bank and the applicable standards before submission.

FCFF = EBIT less tax on EBIT, plus depreciation, less capex, plus change in working capital. Net debt includes working-capital borrowings and, under Ind AS, lease liabilities. Other income is excluded from FCFF. Exit-multiple TV is discounted at year-end.
